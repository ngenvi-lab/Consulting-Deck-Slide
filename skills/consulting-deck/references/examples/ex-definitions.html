APPENDIX · DEFINITIONS AND METHOD

Definitions and method behind every number in this deck

Source and basis

[Report / table]; management basis; USD; as of [date]. [Months] actual, [months] forecast.

Revenue / COGS

Net revenue; cost of goods sold approximated as revenue − gross profit.

Working capital

Receivables + inventory + payables (payables negative). A quarter uses the average of its month-ends.

Days

Receivables ÷ revenue × 365/12; inventory and payables ÷ COGS × 365/12.

Supplier funding

Payables owed ÷ inventory: how much of our stock suppliers fund.

Estimates

Purchases = Δ inventory + COGS; paid to suppliers = opening payables + purchases − closing payables. They ignore write-offs, FX and intercompany moves.

Reference only. Say which figures are reported and which are derived estimates.
